# Inspiration (Mobbin)

Reference screens from Mobbin that shaped the Fides page and app layout, and what we take from each. We borrow patterns, not looks: no colours, logos or layouts are copied.

## Extraction: PDF next to the data

- [Mistral AI — Document AI review](https://mobbin.com/screens/c1b3504b-90d4-444c-a5ff-5e681a49891a): PDF on the left, extracted output on the right, page-by-page blocks with a copy action. **Take:** the split view as the core Fides screen, with the page number on every extracted block.
- [Airwallex — Extracting bill details](https://mobbin.com/screens/7228964b-3706-452e-9e70-2973fbb5e5b9): a small, honest progress state ("This may take up to a minute") over the document. **Take:** say what is happening and how long, e.g. "Reading page 3 of 12".
- [Remote — Add expense](https://mobbin.com/screens/6a8ec814-8c0f-4f9e-a3e3-83dc585cd9d5): every field has helper text explaining what it means. **Take:** each extracted line shows its Czech label and line code under the English name.
- [Midday — document inbox](https://mobbin.com/screens/8c131079-9ff6-44f3-a52e-6bd9958b0b7e): monochrome list of files with a full preview. **Take:** a restrained, near-monochrome document list; colour reserved for status.

## Analysis: ratios and dashboards

- [Origin — Financials and margin trends](https://mobbin.com/screens/87c87777-3d09-4848-9aff-bc390eb11afe): fiscal-year bars, mono uppercase section labels, quiet greens on white. **Take:** mono `label` eyebrows and FY-by-FY comparison charts.
- [Xero Analytics — dashboards](https://mobbin.com/screens/d2a9f37a-bd28-4031-b105-534a1f118dad): every KPI card shows value, comparison period and direction. **Take:** ratio tiles always show the prior year and the change.
- [QuickBooks — Business at a glance](https://mobbin.com/screens/a1576a19-53f6-498d-80a7-02ece64072d8): summary cards with "to review" counts. **Take:** a "3 lines to review" count on the statement card.
- [Stripe — home](https://mobbin.com/screens/54ef3db8-2b9e-4ef3-a91a-cad15de1e1c9): statuses as text pills (Failed) with legends that pair dot and word. **Take:** status always as icon + word.
- [Causal — model dashboard](https://mobbin.com/screens/e639ce92-fadc-48f5-a970-667a7bd3ef62): white canvas, generous spacing, one chart per card. **Take:** one idea per card.

## Brand tone

- [Origin — hero](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/8f04ae13-2a06-4368-8224-4c133eb6ba06): a serif display face that makes finance feel considered, not salesy. **Take:** Newsreader for names and the verdict.
- [Jasper — Trust foundation](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/c2fdca2e-eef2-44a1-bd15-bc269cd09d86): a mono eyebrow over a serif headline, a grid motif for "structure". **Take:** the ledger-row grid in the cover and slides.
- [Duna — compliance](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/46ef263d-c159-4c66-bcec-89f8ebd53c4f): calm, plain claims ("The new standard in compliance"). **Take:** short, factual taglines.
